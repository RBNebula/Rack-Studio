## Integrated demonstration and quality gates

The full twenty-item scene and every workflow in original section 21 remain required. Current examples and the two-cable run-follow fixture are useful validated subsets. They do not demonstrate the required real rail/screw animation, physically secured loom/ties/pad, complete advanced geometry workflow, all reports/formats and final whole-model installation acceptance.

Manual review of actual screenshots covers front and rear mounting contact, an open 1U shelf, the rack, placement, stepped custom example, followed cable curves, master schedule and procurement. There is no completed controlled visual-regression baseline suite. The 42U/40-device/240-port/100-route [synthetic smoke benchmark](benchmarks/geometric-smoke.json) measures CPU geometry/calculation work only; it does not establish browser FPS/memory, dragging targets, physics/loom/imported-geometry stress behaviour or the full performance gate.
